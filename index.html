Skipped items and read errors

DirDelta lists excluded and unreadable items separately. Each entry includes its side, path, reason, and suggested action.

  • Symbolic links: These point to another file or folder. Links are not followed or copied to avoid leaving the selected folders or scanning the same paths repeatedly.
  • Special files: Items that are not regular files or folders, such as sockets, named pipes, and device files, are not compared or copied.
  • Unreadable items: This includes items with insufficient permissions, items deleted or moved during scanning, and items affected by drive errors or drive/network disconnections. Check permissions and connections, then compare again.

Excluded items are not read errors; they are outside the comparison and copying scope. “Results may be incomplete” appears only for read errors: items inside an unreadable folder may not be listed. Copying a folder transfers only items that were successfully scanned. Hidden files and empty folders are normally included.

If a file cannot be read while calculating SHA-256, comparison stops and an error is shown; an unverified file is not treated as identical.
